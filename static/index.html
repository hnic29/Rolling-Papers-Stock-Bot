Backtest Sandbox

Replays the real pullback strategy against actual historical minute-by-minute price data from Alpaca — no real or paper orders are placed, nothing here touches your live account, positions, or trade history.

Two honest limits: historical news and float-share data aren't available, so those two of the five stock-selection criteria never score here (a live scan may flag a stock this backtest wouldn't). And Alpaca's free data feed typically only goes back to around 2020 — "past years" means whatever's actually available, not decades of history. Each run is capped at a 2-year range to keep it running in a reasonable time.
